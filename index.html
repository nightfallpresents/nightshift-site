FAQ

Dress code & entry policy

To maintain a safe, comfortable, and elevated nightlife experience, all guests are expected to arrive appropriately dressed and conduct themselves respectfully.

Not allowed during night events

  • Sweatpants, joggers, or gym shorts
  • Work boots or steel-toe footwear
  • Clothing displaying offensive, threatening, or inappropriate graphics or messaging
  • Excessive chains, spikes, or accessories presenting safety concerns
  • Ski masks, balaclavas, or identity-concealing face coverings, except for religious purposes and applicable accommodations
  • Clothing or accessories that interfere with security screening
  • Excessively dirty or soiled clothing

Additional entry policies

Management reserves the right to deny entry or remove guests for dress code violations, disruptive behavior, excessive intoxication, safety concerns, or failure to comply with venue policies.

Aggressive conduct, harassment, threats, fighting, and disrespect toward staff or other guests will not be tolerated.

Dress code requirements may vary depending on the event, artist, or venue programming. Guests are encouraged to arrive in attire appropriate for an elevated nightlife environment.

When in doubt, dress to impress.

All entry and removal decisions are made by venue management and security in accordance with applicable law. Policies are enforced consistently and without discrimination.

Bag policy

To ensure a safe and efficient entry experience, all guests must comply with the following bag policy.

Permitted bags

  • Small purses, handbags, and clutches
  • Small crossbody bags and fanny packs
  • Bags no larger than 12" × 6" × 12"

Prohibited bags

  • Backpacks and large bookbags
  • Duffel bags, luggage, and oversized totes
  • Large purses or bags exceeding the permitted dimensions
  • Bags containing prohibited or hazardous items
  • Any bag that cannot be properly inspected by security

General Information

What is the age requirement?

All Night Shift events are 18+. You must be at least 18 years old to attend.

Guests must present valid identification upon entry. Guests 21 and older must provide acceptable identification to purchase or consume alcohol.

Do I need an ID?

Yes. All guests must present valid identification to enter, regardless of age.

We accept:

  • Driver's licenses
  • State-issued IDs
  • Passports
  • Apple Wallet digital IDs, where supported by our ID verification system

Don't have your physical ID?

A photograph or screenshot of your ID may be accepted on a case-by-case basis, subject to management approval and applicable law.

Acceptance is not guaranteed. Security may request additional verification or deny entry if your identity or age cannot be verified.

We strongly recommend bringing your physical ID to avoid issues at the door.

Photographs and screenshots may not satisfy identification requirements for purchasing alcohol.

What time do doors open?

Door times vary by event and are listed on the event's Posh ticket page. We recommend arriving early to avoid long lines.

Can I leave and come back?

Re-entry policies may vary by event. Please check with security before leaving the venue.

Tickets & Admission

What are ticket tiers?

Ticket tiers are simply different prices for the exact same General Admission ticket.

Here's how it works:

  • Early Bird: The cheapest tickets. These go on sale first, and only a limited number are available.
  • General Admission (GA): Regular-priced tickets available after Early Bird tickets sell out.
  • Final Release / Last Minute: The most expensive tickets, usually available closer to the event or after the cheaper tiers sell out.

For example:

  • Early Bird — $15
  • General Admission — $20
  • Final Release — $25

All three tickets get you into the exact same event with the exact same access.

Someone who purchases an Early Bird ticket for $15 gets the same General Admission experience as someone who pays $25 for Final Release.

The only difference is the price. Buy early and save money.

VIP tickets, when offered, are separate and may include additional benefits.

Why did ticket prices increase?

Because the cheaper ticket tier sold out!

As each tier sells out, the next tier becomes available at a higher price. The earlier you purchase, the cheaper your ticket usually is.

Where can I purchase tickets?

All official Night Shift tickets are sold through Posh.

We strongly recommend purchasing tickets through our official event pages to avoid scams or invalid tickets.

Can I buy tickets at the door?

Door tickets may be available if the event has not sold out.

Advance purchase is strongly recommended. Door admission is not guaranteed and may cost more.

Are tickets refundable?

All ticket sales are generally final.

Refunds are not provided for changes in personal plans, missed performances, late arrivals, or entry denied due to violations of event policies.

Canceled or rescheduled events are handled according to Posh's policies and applicable law.

Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?

Yes! If you can no longer attend, you can give your ticket to someone else.

All Night Shift tickets are issued through Posh. You can share or transfer your ticket to another person, but each ticket can only be scanned and used once.

The person receiving your ticket must still meet the event's 18+ age requirement and all entry policies.

Do I need to print my ticket?

No! Simply have your digital Posh ticket ready on your phone when you arrive.

Make sure your phone is charged and your ticket is accessible before reaching the entrance.

Does purchasing a ticket guarantee entry?

A valid ticket provides admission as long as you meet the age requirements, present acceptable identification, comply with event policies, and pass security screening.

Admission remains subject to venue capacity and applicable law.

What happens if an event sells out?

Once an event sells out, additional tickets are not guaranteed.

Any additional ticket releases will be announced through our official channels.

Entry & Security

Will I be searched when I enter?

Yes. All guests are subject to security screening, which may include metal detectors, bag inspections, and other safety procedures.

Guests who refuse required security screening may be denied entry.

Can I bring outside food or drinks?

Outside food and beverages are generally not permitted.

Exceptions may be made for medically necessary items and other required accommodations.

Can I be denied entry or removed?

Yes. Management and security may deny entry or remove guests for:

  • Excessive intoxication or impairment
  • Fighting, threats, or aggressive behavior
  • Harassment or unwanted physical contact
  • Failure to comply with security screening
  • Violations of event or venue policies
  • Behavior that threatens the safety of guests or staff

Guests removed for violating event policies are generally not eligible for refunds, subject to applicable law.

What should I do if I feel unsafe?

Immediately notify a security guard or member of our staff.

Night Shift takes all reports of harassment, threatening behavior, and safety concerns seriously.

Our priority is maintaining a safe and enjoyable environment for everyone.

Event Experience

When will set times be announced?

Set times are typically announced through our official social media channels before the event.

Some events may not release set times until the day of the show. All schedules are subject to change.

Can I take photos or videos?

Personal photography and video recording using mobile phones are generally permitted unless otherwise stated.

Some events may enforce specific no-phone or no-recording policies. Please respect the rules for each event.

Can I bring professional camera or video equipment?

Professional photography and videography equipment is strictly prohibited without a pre-approved media pass and a valid Certificate of Insurance (COI).

If you're interested in photographing or filming a Night Shift event, please contact our team in advance to request media credentials and submit the required documentation.

Showing up with professional camera equipment without prior approval will result in your equipment being denied entry.

Media passes must be approved before the event. Approval is not guaranteed, and all media personnel must follow Night Shift's photography, videography, and event policies.

Is there a coat check?

Coat check availability varies by event and venue.

Please refer to the event information or ask staff upon arrival.

Will alcohol be available?

Alcohol availability depends on the venue and applicable licensing.

Guests must be 21 or older and provide legally acceptable identification to purchase or consume alcoholic beverages.

Can I upgrade to VIP?

VIP upgrades may be available at select events, subject to availability.

If VIP admission is offered, the details will be listed on the event's Posh ticket page.

Are events accessible?

Accessibility features vary by venue.

If you require accessibility accommodations, please contact us ahead of time so our team can help coordinate your visit.

Contact & Support

I lost something at an event. What should I do?

Please contact the venue or Night Shift with a description of your missing item and the date of the event.

We will do our best to assist but cannot guarantee the recovery of lost belongings.

I can't find my tickets. What should I do?

All Night Shift tickets are issued through Posh.

Check your email inbox and spam folder for your ticket confirmation, or log into your Posh account to access your tickets.

If you're still unable to locate them, please contact Posh support for assistance.

I accidentally purchased tickets for the wrong event. What should I do?

All ticket sales are generally final.

If you purchased a ticket for the wrong event, contact Posh support to see whether any options are available.

Ticket exchanges and refunds are not guaranteed.

How do I contact Night Shift?

For general inquiries, ticket questions, media requests, accessibility accommodations, or event-related concerns, please reach out through our official website or social media channels.

Email contact@nightfallpresents.com or use the contact form.
